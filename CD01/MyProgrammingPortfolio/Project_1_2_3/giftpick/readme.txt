		Welcome to Keith's Christmas Gift Giving program.
		This is a beta release. In short, USE AT YOUR OWN RISK.
		I assume no responsibility, but I will gladly take a donation.

		The general idea of the program is to eliminate the hat and
		names on pieces of paper method of selecting who buys whom a
		gift for Christmas. By simply entering the names of all those
		participating in the gift giving, one may then calculate who
		gives to whom with the Create List button.

		Further versions may include stealth mode. This would be for
		those who like the surprise in not knowing until Christmas
		who their special gift giver is. Just think of it. Everyone
		huddled around the computer anxiously awaiting their turn to
		see who the computer program has lovingly selected for them.
